**Daniel wants to want his life.**

He loves his wife. He’s good at his job. He can enjoy a meal, a joke, a song. Then the moment passes, and he’s looking for a way through the hours that remain.

Recovery has given him back trust, routines, and a future. He goes to meetings. He stays sober. But after another relapse, he wonders how long he can keep getting through a life he’s supposed to be enjoying.

A brain implant offers something he’s almost stopped hoping for: **enough reason to stay.** With a book. With a conversation. With an ordinary Saturday night.

It works.

But his wife still wants a different future. The problems at work still need changing. And the discontent that once pushed him to act has grown quiet.

**If you can be happy where you are, how do you know when it’s time to leave?**
