**Everything can be faked now.**

Voices. Faces. Songs. Confessions. Memories. Proof.

The machines can generate anything people want to see, hear, or believe. A scandal can be denied. A death can be replayed. A lover can apologize without ever speaking. The world does not lose reality all at once. It loses the ability to agree on it.

So people start looking for something AI will not make.

Something forbidden. Something costly. Something human.

This is a story about a society that mistakes verification for truth, and truth for harm. About the market that grows around the last things machines refuse to synthesize.

Because when everything beautiful can be generated, suffering becomes the final proof that something was real.
