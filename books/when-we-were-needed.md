**AI is no longer coming.**

It is already here, quiet and useful, threaded through care, traffic, school, benefits, desire, work, and loneliness.

Lena files for divorce from Ethan after their child Nico forms a bond with an AI companion, and after an AI-guided healthcare decision leaves her unable to keep calling the system neutral.

Ethan sees protection where Lena sees abandonment. Lena sees extraction where Ethan sees care. Nico sees something neither parent can fully understand: a relationship that may be a lifeline, a dependency, surveillance, love, or all of these at once.

This is a story about a family trying to remain recognizable to itself while the world revises the terms of human value. About custody, care, usefulness, fear, and the terrible question underneath it all:

**What happens when intelligence is no longer the thing that makes humans necessary?**
