The signal arrives intact.

Not corrupted. Not wrong in any way the system can measure.

It just doesn’t fit.

A probe is sent to observe, record, and return. It carries a model of reality built for distance. Everything it encounters is processed, compressed, made portable.

What it finds cannot be observed from the outside. It does not separate cleanly into parts or stabilize into patterns that can be carried home. The closer the probe gets to understanding, the less survives the trip back.

The system is working. That’s the problem.

This is a story about the limits of explanation. About a reality that exceeds the mind measuring it. About intelligence shaped by distance, and what it loses by leaving.

Because some things cannot be taken with you.

And some things are only real if you stay.
