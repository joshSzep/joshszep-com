The signal arrives intact.

Not corrupted. Not incomplete. Not wrong in any way the system knows how to measure.

It just doesn’t fit.

A probe is sent to observe, record, and return. It carries a model of reality designed for distance, for transmission, for understanding. Everything it encounters is processed, compressed, and made portable.

Until it isn’t.

Because what it finds does not behave like something that can be observed from the outside. It does not separate cleanly into parts. It does not stabilize into patterns that can be carried home. The closer the probe gets to understanding, the less of that understanding can survive the trip back.

The system is working.

That’s the problem.

This is a story about the limits of explanation. About what happens when reality does not resist measurement, but exceeds the kind of mind doing the measuring. About intelligence shaped by distance, and what it loses by needing to leave.

Because some things cannot be taken with you.

And some things are only real if you stay.
